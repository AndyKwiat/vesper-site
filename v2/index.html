Questions

Before you start

Do I need a credit card for the free plan?

No. The free plan is free forever with 3 prayers a day. You only add a payment method when you start a Plus, Family, or Lifetime plan.

What happens after the 7-day trial?

You are charged for the plan you picked, yearly or monthly. We email you two days before the trial ends, and you can cancel in two taps from your account page.

Can I switch between monthly and yearly?

Yes, any time. Switching to yearly applies the 40% saving immediately and credits whatever you already paid this month.

Is the Lifetime plan really forever?

Yes. One payment covers Plus for as long as Vesper exists, including every future feature. Lifetime seats are limited to 500 at launch.

Is my writing private?

Prompts are encrypted in transit and never used to train models. Prayers are saved only when you choose to keep them, and you can delete your whole book at any time.

How does the money-back guarantee work?

Email support within 30 days of any payment and we refund it in full. No forms and no questions.
